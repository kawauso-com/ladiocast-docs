# Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

Ladiocast is provided by kawauso.com as a free app, at no cost, and is intended for use as is.

This page informs you of my policies regarding the collection, use and disclosure of personal information when you use Ladiocast, including the alpha, beta and release candidate versions distributed through TestFlight.

## Information collection and use

Ladiocast does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal information. All settings, such as audio device selections, mixer levels and streaming destinations, are stored only on your Mac.

## Microphone and audio input

Ladiocast accesses the microphone and other audio input devices on your Mac, only after you grant permission, in order to mix the audio and stream it. The audio is processed in real time on your Mac and sent only to the streaming server(s) that you configure in the app (for example, an Icecast, SHOUTcast or RTMP server). Ladiocast does not record audio to disk and does not send audio to kawauso.com or any other third party.

## System audio recording (Loopback Streamer)

When you select the Loopback Streamer, Ladiocast uses the system audio recording of macOS, only after you grant permission, in order to pass the audio you mix to other applications on your Mac through a virtual input device named "Ladiocast Loopback". Ladiocast captures only its own audio output for this purpose, not the audio of other applications. The audio stays on your Mac: Ladiocast does not record it to disk and does not send it to kawauso.com or any other third party. The applications that you choose to receive the audio are subject to their own privacy practices.

## Streaming destinations

The server address, port, user name, password, stream name and other metadata that you enter are used solely to connect to the streaming server you specify. They are stored on your Mac only and are not transmitted to kawauso.com. The streaming servers you connect to, and the listeners of your stream, are outside of my control and are subject to their own privacy practices.

## Log data

Ladiocast does not collect log data, analytics or crash reports, and contains no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs. If you use Apple's TestFlight, Apple may collect crash reports and feedback according to Apple's privacy policy and your own TestFlight settings; kawauso.com receives them only if you choose to share them.

## Feedback

If you send feedback through GitHub Issues, your post and your GitHub account name are public. Please do not include passwords, stream keys or server addresses you want to keep private. If you write to me by email, I use your message and address only to reply to you.

## Changes to this Privacy Policy

I may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes are effective as soon as they are posted on this page, so please review it periodically.
